About the Book

The Law of Dependent Arising: The Secret of Bondage and Release is the English translation of Ven. Katukurunde Nanananda Thero's twenty-sermon exposition of Paticca Samuppada (Dependent Arising), originally delivered as Sermons 183–202 of the Pahankanuwa sermon series.

Inspired by a wish to clarify the profound teaching of Dependent Arising through the Buddha's own discourses, Ven. Nanananda devoted this series entirely to the themes of conditionality, bondage, and liberation. The work expands and develops many of the insights introduced in his renowned Nibbana - The Mind Stilled sermons and represents one of most sustained examinations of the Buddha's central teaching on dependent arising.

About Ven. Katukurunde Nanananda Thero

Ven. Katukurunde Nanananda Mahathera (1940–2018) was a Sri Lankan Buddhist monk, Pali scholar, and meditator widely respected for his original and thought-provoking expositions of the Buddha's teachings.

Among his best-known works are Concept and Reality, Nibbana - The Mind Stilled, and The Law of Dependent Arising: The Secret of Bondage and Release. His teachings place particular emphasis on direct insight into experience, dependent arising, emptiness, and the nature of liberation as presented in the early Buddhist texts.
